About Re:Peat

Re:Peat was created by a guy who wants to study more, without the friction of opening an app. We've all been there; you make your Anki(or any spaced-repetion service) decks, add a bunch of cards, and for the first few months or even years, you keep a consistent studying pace. But as time goes on, life gets busy and even opening an app to study becomes somewhat of a chore or forgotten habit.

The goal of Re:Peat is to help turn casual habits, like opening new tabs, into learning sessions. It's been proven time and time again, that systems are more effective than motivation or discipline, and Re:Peat leverages this principle to create a seamless learning experience built on top of your web surfing.

Whether you're studying for exams, learning a new language, or mastering professional skills, Re:Peat transforms your browsing habits into a powerful learning companion.
